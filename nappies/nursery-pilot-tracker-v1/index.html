Help & Guidance

How to use this tracker

  1. Complete the Baseline section before the pilot starts.
  2. Complete the Weekly Log once per week.
  3. Use Issues & Risks whenever something needs support or escalation.
  4. Use Reports & Downloads to export the data for RNfL, NLWA, or borough reporting.

Data collection note

Do not enter children’s names. This tracker is designed for aggregate nursery-level information only.
