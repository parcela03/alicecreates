Mini Diary

Write one or two sentences in Spanish. Use the prompts below.

Today

Hoy he... = Today I have...

Hoy he limpiado la cocina.
Today I have cleaned the kitchen.

Yesterday

Ayer... usually uses the preterite.

Ayer limpié la cocina.
Yesterday I cleaned the kitchen.

Habit / background

Cuando era... often uses the imperfect.

Cuando era niña, tocaba la guitarra.
When I was a child, I used to play guitar.

Time Expressions

Practise using Spanish time expressions with the right tense.

Your sentence

Useful rule: Time expressions often help you choose the tense. Hoy often uses the present perfect when the result matters now. Ayer usually uses the preterite. Normalmente usually uses the present. Cuando era pequeña often uses the imperfect.

Days of the Week Reference

In Spanish, days of the week are usually written in lowercase.

English Spanish Example
Monday lunes el lunes / los lunes
Tuesday martes el martes / los martes
Wednesday miércoles el miércoles / los miércoles
Thursday jueves el jueves / los jueves
Friday viernes el viernes / los viernes
Saturday sábado el sábado / los sábados
Sunday domingo el domingo / los domingos
